    06Operating principles

    What I optimize for.

    P·01

    Reliability by default

    Systems are designed for the day something breaks — retries, timeouts, and graceful degradation are features, not afterthoughts.

    P·02

    Automate the toil

    If a task happens twice, it becomes a script. If it happens weekly, it becomes a pipeline.

    P·03

    Observability built in

    Logs, metrics, and traces from day one. You can't operate what you can't see.

    P·04

    Simple until proven otherwise

    Boring technology wins. Complexity is admitted only when scale demands it.
